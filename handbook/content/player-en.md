# Welcome, Player

This manual is for **you, the player** — the person holding the phone
during the game. You don't need an account, you don't install
anything: the Gamemaster (GM) hands you a QR code or a short URL, you
open it, pick a team name, and you're in.

There are two game types and the manual covers both:

- **Part 1, MiSSiONS games.** A classic photo/video scavenger hunt
  at a single location. Your team works through a list of missions
  the GM curated; you upload media for each, the GM accepts or
  rejects, you collect points.
- **Part 2, CityRush games.** A GPS-driven mode. The map shows the
  next stop; your team physically walks to it, the app unlocks the
  task on arrival, and you submit on the spot. Plus optional
  "specials" you can do any time and a PvP freeze mechanic.

You won't always know in advance which type you're playing — both
start the same way, with a QR code from the GM. Just follow the
on-screen flow and the right manual section will be obvious.

> Screenshots show a demo game. Your real games will look the same
> with your own team name and the missions the GM picked.

::part:1:Playing MiSSiONS games

# Joining the game

::shot:m-01-join

After scanning the QR code (or opening the URL the GM gave you), this
is the very first screen. From top to bottom:

- The big stenciled **MiSSiONS** wordmark and the **GAME · …**
  short code so you can confirm with the GM you joined the right
  game.
- A round **Tap to capture** target — tap it to take a team selfie
  with the front camera. The selfie shows up next to your team name
  on the live rankings, so put a face to your team. (You can skip
  this and add it later, but most teams take it now.)
- **Team name** — type your team name in the field. Pick something
  recognizable; the GM and other teams see this exact string.
- **Language** — DE / EN / FR / IT / ES. Pick yours; you can change
  it later from inside the game. The GM may have restricted the
  list to the languages the missions are translated into.

Tap **Join game →** at the bottom and you're in.

# Joining a team that already exists

::shot:m-02-join-existing

If your group is more than two or three people, you'll often want
**two phones on the same team** — one person carries the camera
phone, another handles chat and reviewing. Or someone joined first
on their own, then realized you want to play together.

For these cases, tap **Or join an existing team** on the join screen.
This panel lists every team already in the game with their current
score. Tap **Join** next to your team and your phone hooks into that
team's session — same missions, same chat, same score. Each phone
can still submit independently.

This link only appears once at least one team has already joined the
game; before that you only see the create-new flow.

# The player home

::shot:m-03-home

Once you've joined, this is your home base. The top bar shows:

- **Score** (top-left) — your team's current point total.
- **MiSSiONS** wordmark in the middle with the game timer right
  below it, plus your team name in small grey letters.
- **Chat** / **Rules** / **Language** icons on the right. The chat
  icon gets an orange dot when the GM has written to you.

Below the bar:

- **Filter strip** — Photo / Video / Indoor toggles. Hide categories
  you don't want to see right now.
- **Mission cards** — one per mission. Each card shows the mission
  name, a short description, the task, the points on offer, and a
  status pill (Open / Awaiting review / Done / Rejected).

Tap any card to open its detail sheet.

# Submitting a mission

::shot:m-04-mission-detail

Tapping a card slides up a sheet with everything you need:

- **Mission name and points** at the top.
- **Description** — flavour text, what this mission is about.
- **Task** — what the GM literally wants you to do. Read this
  carefully; the GM rejects uploads that miss the task.
- **Take photo / Take video** — opens the camera in the right mode
  for this mission.
- **Upload from gallery** — pick a photo or video you already
  took. Useful if the action moment passed before you opened the
  app.

Once you submit, the card flips to **Awaiting review** and the GM
sees it on their dashboard. They either tap Accept (and you get the
points) or Reject with a reason — in which case the mission becomes
available again and you can retry.

Close the sheet by tapping the × in the top-right or the dark
backdrop.

# Chat with the Gamemaster

::shot:m-05-chat

Tap the chat icon (top-right of the home view) to switch to the chat
panel. It's a one-on-one thread between your team and the GM —
other teams cannot see it.

Use it for:

- Asking the GM to clarify a task you don't understand.
- Reporting that a mission location is closed/blocked.
- Reading rejection reasons after the GM rejects an upload (these
  arrive here as well as on the mission card).
- Reading **broadcasts** the GM sent to everyone — these have a
  small "Broadcast" tag so you know they weren't aimed only at
  you.

Type, tap **Send**, and you're done. Tap **Back** in the topbar to
get back to your missions.

::part:2:Playing CityRush games

CityRush is the GPS-driven game type. Instead of running a list of
photo missions in one place, your team physically walks between
stops on a map. The next stop is shown; you arrive, the task
unlocks, you submit, you move on. Specials and a PvP freeze
mechanic add a layer on top.

The screens look similar to MiSSiONS but include a map and a couple
of CityRush-only widgets.

# The CityRush home

::shot:cr-01-home

The CityRush player home replaces the MiSSiONS mission list with
something more spatial. Top to bottom:

- **Topbar** — score on the left, game timer in the middle, chat /
  rules / language on the right. Same idea as MiSSiONS.
- **Progress bar** — `STOP n / N` and the CityRush mode name.
- **Mini-map** — your team's position (the orange dot) plus every
  mission stop. Tap the map to expand it fullscreen.
- **Mission tiles** in route order. Each tile shows the stop number,
  the mission name (or `??` if you haven't arrived yet), points on
  offer, and a status pill.
- **Gold star button** at the bottom — special-mission shortcut,
  covered below.

Tap a tile to open the same mission detail sheet you saw in
MiSSiONS. The submission flow (take photo / video / upload from
gallery) is identical.

# The special-mission button

::shot:cr-02-special-btn

This gold round star sits permanently at the bottom-center of the
screen as long as there's **exactly one** special mission left.
Specials are bonus missions playable any time, anywhere — no GPS
requirement, often a quick group task (a dance, a chant, a
funny pose). Tap the star to open the special directly.

If your route has **two or more** specials, they instead surface as
gold-glowing tiles at the top of the tile grid and the round button
disappears. If there are **zero**, the button is hidden.

# The fullscreen map

::shot:cr-03-fullscreen-map

Tap the mini-map at the top of the home view to expand it to the
full screen. You see:

- **Every mission stop** as a marker, in route order.
- **Your team's position** as a moving dot (only if you granted
  location permission).
- The **gold star button** at the bottom-center stays visible so you
  can launch a special from inside the map too.
- A **JUMP TO ME** chip top-right that recenters the map on your
  current position.
- A **BACK / ZURÜCK** chip top-left to return to the mission view.

The fullscreen map is for orientation. To submit, go back to the
mission view and tap a tile.

# Getting frozen

::shot:cr-04-frozen

If a rival team caught you on camera and the GM accepted their
freeze, you'll see a big blue **FROZEN** banner at the top with a
countdown, the mission view tints cold-blue, and an "You have been
caught!" toast appears at the bottom.

While frozen:

- You **cannot submit** missions (Take photo / Upload buttons are
  locked behind a frost overlay).
- The game timer keeps running — freezes are real time, you don't
  get back the minutes you lost.
- You can still **browse** missions, read the chat, and look at the
  map.

When the freeze timer ends, the banner disappears, the tint clears,
and the submission buttons unlock automatically. The GM can also
**thaw** you early if the situation warrants it.

To avoid being frozen: don't pose for photos with rival teams in
sight, and switch routes if you spot one tailing you. (Yes,
CityRush is partly a stealth game.)

## End-of-game

When the GM ends the game or the timer runs out:

- A modal pops up with the GM's end-of-game message and your final
  score.
- The mission view goes read-only — submissions are no longer
  accepted.
- Your final selfie and the team's submitted photos may end up in
  a slideshow the GM renders for the prize ceremony.

You can keep the page open to look at the rankings, but the
"working" part of the game is over.

## Tips that win games

- **Read the task, not just the title.** The card title is short;
  the task line right above the submit buttons is the actual
  rubric the GM rejects against.
- **Submit clear photos.** A blurry shot or one without the
  required subject in frame is the #1 reason for rejections.
  Frame the subject, hold the phone steady, tap.
- **Use chat early.** If a mission is impossible because the
  location is closed or the prop is missing, message the GM right
  away instead of grinding on it. They can swap or remove it.
- **Watch your battery.** GPS in CityRush + camera in both modes
  drain the battery fast. Carry a power bank.
- **In CityRush, do specials between stops, not at stops.** Stops
  unlock when you arrive; doing a special exactly there wastes the
  geofence dwell time.

## Common questions

**The Take photo button is greyed out.**
Either you're frozen (see the FROZEN banner) or the mission was
already accepted. If neither, ask the GM in chat.

**My score didn't update after I submitted.**
Submission ≠ accept. The score only ticks up after the GM accepts.
You'll see the mission flip from "Awaiting review" to "Done".

**I joined the wrong game.**
Tell the GM; you can scan the right QR code from scratch. The wrong
game's session forgets you as soon as the GM ends it (or after
24 hours of inactivity).

**My team's selfie is wrong.**
On the join screen the selfie is the team selfie. If you want to
re-take it, the GM has to delete the team and you re-join — or use
the "Or join an existing team" link with a fresh device, then
overwrite the selfie at upload time (your selfie capture overwrites
the team's existing one).

**I'm on iOS and the camera shows a black screen.**
iOS Safari requires you to grant camera permission in Settings →
Safari → Camera (set to "Ask" or "Allow"). After granting, refresh
the page.

**A CityRush mission stays locked even though I'm at the spot.**
Your GPS accuracy might be poor. Step out of the building, into the
open sky, and wait 5–10 seconds. If it still doesn't unlock, ask the
GM to manually mark the arrival from their dashboard.
